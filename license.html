License & Usage

Last updated: November 2026

Draft policy template. This text is a starting point written for a small independent studio. Review it with a qualified legal professional and update it to match how you actually operate before launch.

What you can do

  • Use purchased assets in your own personal or commercial projects (videos, ads, social posts, client work) unless the product page says otherwise.
  • Modify and combine assets with your own work.

What you can't do

  • Resell, share, redistribute or upload the original files (alone or in a bundle) to marketplaces or file-sharing sites.
  • Claim to have created the original files, or use them to build a competing asset pack.

Seats

One licence covers one buyer. Teams should buy one licence per person or contact us for a team licence.

Per-product terms

Some products (for example wallpaper collections) may be personal-use only. The product page always states the licence.

AI-generated content

Copyright treatment of AI-generated material varies by country. We make no guarantee of exclusive ownership of AI-generated output.

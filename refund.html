Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Draft policy template. This text is a starting point written for a small independent studio. Review it with a qualified legal professional and update it to match how you actually operate before launch.

Digital products

Because digital files can't be returned, purchases are generally non-refundable once delivered. We will fix or refund a purchase if:

  • the files are faulty, incomplete or can't be opened, and we can't fix it;
  • the product is materially different from its description;
  • you were charged twice for the same order.

Contact us within 7 days of purchase with your order reference and a description of the problem.

Failed or duplicate payments

If money was taken but no order was confirmed, contact us with your payment reference. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

Custom projects

Deposits, milestones and refunds for custom work are set out in your written quote.
